A Parent's Guide: fostering connections in the acting world

Aurora Theatre — 7 Jun 2024

We are back with another article for you, parents of young talents- how’s your week going?

You certainly know that networking and community building play an important role in people’s lives, but what can they do for young performers?

We have the answer: networking is vital even from a young age.

By connecting with others, you and your child can gain valuable support, opportunities, and valuable resources.

This week, we’ll explore the importance of networking and provide practical tips for building relationships within the acting community. Ready? Let’s dive in!

1. Join local theatre groups:

Local theatre groups are important for young performers to develop their skills. They are also great to gain experience and connect with others who share their interests. Encouraging children to take part in community theatre productions, workshops, and classes can help them expand their talents. Furthermore, they can form relationships with fellow actors, directors, and mentors. Involvement in local theatre groups offers parents opportunities to connect with other families, share experiences, and exchange resources.

2. Attending Workshops and Events:

Attending workshops, seminars, and industry events is an excellent way for young performers to learn from professionals. It is also a great way to get exposure to different aspects of the industry and expand their network. Look for workshops or masterclasses led by reputable acting coaches, casting directors, and industry professionals that cater to children and young actors. These events provide valuable learning opportunities and networking possibilities for both children and parents.

3. Connecting with Other Families:

Building relationships with other families in similar situations can provide a sense of camaraderie, support, and understanding. Seek out opportunities to connect with other parents and caregivers of young performers, whether it’s through local theater groups, online forums, or social media groups. Sharing experiences, tips, and resources with other families can help navigate the challenges and joys of raising a young actor.

4. Utilizing Online Platforms:

Online platforms such as social media (Instagram and Facebook Groups), casting websites (check out Backstage, Casting Networks, Actor’s Access) , and actor networking sites (TCG and YAP ) offer additional places for networking and community building within the acting community. Create a professional profile for your child on casting websites and participate in online acting forums. It’s a great way to connect with industry professionals and peers. Parents can also use social media platforms to network with casting directors and other industry professionals. Remember to stay updated on audition opportunities and industry news.

5. Volunteering and Collaborating:

Volunteering and collaborating with local theatres, schools, or community organizations can provide valuable networking opportunities and hands-on experience for young performers. Volunteer with your young ones for backstage roles or assist with set construction. Participate in community outreach programs. Expand the network and gain exposure to different aspects of the theatre community. Collaborating on creative projects with peers fosters teamwork, creativity, and camaraderie within the acting community.

Networking and community building are essential components of success in the acting world, both for children and parents. By joining local theatre groups, attending workshops and events, connecting with other families, using online platforms, volunteering, and collaborating, young performers can expand their network. They can gain valuable support, and foster meaningful relationships within the acting community.

Remember, building connections is not only about advancing one’s career but also about building a supportive community that nurtures and celebrates the talents of young actors.
