Drama game of the week: freeze

Aurora Theatre — 30 Nov 2023

It’s drama game time!!!! How are you doing today?

We had a very exciting week performing one of our Murder Mystery Dinner Shows, and because we are in the mood for improv, we are going to shine a spotlight on the classic improv game called “Freeze.” We played this game multiple times in our acting classes and every time we were in for a treat. And speaking of treats: if you are in Hong Kong you should totally check out our acting classes because they are awesome, our acting teachers are awesome, and we have awesome stuff for you to learn.

Find out more here.

Whether you’re a seasoned performer or a drama enthusiast looking for a fun activity, Freeze is sure to add a burst of creativity to your theatrical journey;  a dynamic and spontaneous improv game that encourages quick thinking and creativity.

Here’s how it works:

1. Two players begin a scene based on a suggestion or theme.

2. At any point during the scene, an offstage voice can shout “Freeze!” prompting the players to instantly freeze in their current positions.

3. The player who called “Freeze” taps one of the frozen performers, takes their position, and starts a completely new scene inspired by the frozen poses.

4. The cycle repeats, creating a chain of interconnected scenes that evolve in unexpected and hilarious ways.

The benefits of playing this game are multiple: for example, it encourages spontaneity as it sharpens your ability to think on your feet and respond quickly to unexpected situations.

It also encourages teamwork as it fosters collaboration as players build on each other’s ideas, creating a seamless flow of scenes.

Last but not least, it nourishes creativity: the ever-changing scenarios challenge your imagination, pushing you to explore diverse characters and storylines.

As you go along with the game, remember:

1. Embrace the Absurd: Don’t be afraid to take risks and embrace the unexpected. The more absurd the scenes, the more entertaining the game becomes.

2. Active Listening: Pay close attention to your scene partner and the overall narrative. This helps in smoothly transitioning between scenes and maintaining coherence.

3. Encourage Everyone: Inclusion is key. Ensure that everyone gets a chance to participate, fostering a supportive and inclusive atmosphere.

In the world of drama games, Freeze stands out as a versatile and entertaining choice. Whether you’re a drama teacher looking for a new activity for your students or a group of friends seeking a lively game night, Freeze promises laughter, creativity, and memorable moments. Gather your fellow performers, unleash your imagination, and let the scenes unfold in this exhilarating Drama Game of the Week!
