Drama game of the Week: Delving into Backstories

Aurora Theatre — 19 Oct 2023

For aspiring actors, the ability to bring characters to life with depth and authenticity is crucial. Character Confessions offers a unique approach to character development, allowing acting students to explore the rich backstories of the characters they portray, resulting in more engaging and genuine performances.

Here are the steps:

Character selection: In this activity, each acting student begins by selecting a character from a play, movie, or story that they are working on. This character will serve as the focal point of their exploration.

Solo reflection: Students are encouraged to reflect on their chosen character’s life before the events of the play or story. They should consider the character’s upbringing, significant life events, relationships, and hidden secrets. This exploration is the character’s backstory.

Confession writing: Students are then prompted to write a confession from the perspective of their chosen character. This confession can be a letter, diary entry, or monologue, in which the character reveals a significant aspect of their past that the audience is unaware of.

Sharing and performance: In the classroom, students can take turns performing their character’s confession in front of their peers. During their performance, they must embody the character’s emotions and persona while delivering the confession.

Discussion: a group discussion follows each performance. Students are encouraged to discuss how delving into their character’s backstory enhanced their understanding of the character and influenced their performance choices.

This game enhances character development by delving into the character’s past while deepening the emotional connection to the character and promoting creative writing and improvisational skills. It also fosters empathy and a better understanding of the character’s motivations.

Character Confessions is a captivating storytelling activity that empowers acting students to infuse their performances with depth and authenticity.

Students can establish a profound connection with the characters they portray by allowing them to confess their innermost secrets and explore their backstories. This results in more captivating, emotionally resonant, and engaging performances.

Incorporate this activity into your acting classes, and witness your students’ performances evolving with newfound dimensions of authenticity and depth.
