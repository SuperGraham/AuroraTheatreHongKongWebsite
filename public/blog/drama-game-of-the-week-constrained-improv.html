Drama game of the week: Constrained improv

Aurora Theatre — 22 Nov 2023

Hello everyone! Great to be back with you all; we hope your week was nothing short of amazing.

We are back with another game and another challenge for your exciting drama classroom/rehearsal space, and today we have something for you to play with improv for emotional expression.

Ready for what’s coming this week? We want to challenge your actor students this week to enhance emotional expression and choice commitment, and what’s better than constrained improv to do that?

Constrained Improv is a sophisticated adult drama game designed for actors (but it can, of course, be used for younger students- you just need to create scenarios that are accessible to them). It emphasises its focus on intricate scenarios, complex characters, and challenging constraints.

As you set the stage, discuss with your students the crucial role of scenario setting, providing examples like strained family gatherings or intense business negotiations. Explain how detailed character assignments contribute to a deeper exploration of emotions and relationships.

Keep adding complexity with constraints: introduce different ones such as silent scenes, monologue-only communication, and reverse emotions. Highlight the impact of constraints in pushing actors to think on their feet and deliver nuanced performances.

Observe how dynamic changes and adaptation occur: explore the concept of introducing dynamic changes during the improv, keeping actors on their toes, and discuss the importance of adaptability and how unexpected elements contribute to the overall challenge.

Use the next step to debrief and reflect: stress the significance of post-improv reflection for actors to learn from challenges and successes.

Encourage actors to discuss their experiences, what worked well, and how constraints influenced their creative choices. Constrained Improv is a great activity to incorporate for theatre practitioners into their training repertoire and it deeply unlocks emotional depth and enhances actor skills.

Among the various actor training exercises, this is one of the boldest and strongest ones.

Have you tried this game before, or is it the first time you are incorporating it into your classroom?

Don’t forget to share your experience in the comment section and sign up for our mailing list for more content and updates!

See you next week!
