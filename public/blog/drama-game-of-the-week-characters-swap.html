Drama game of the week: Characters Swap

Aurora Theatre — 10 Oct 2023

Welcome back to our Drama Game of the Week series! We hope you enjoyed last week’s game and that your students had a fun time.

Remember to share any feedback, story, or anything that can help other people with your unique experience; we are always curious to know what’s going on with you.

This week, we have another exciting and creative game called “Character Swap” This activity will challenge your students’ improvisational skills and character development. Whether you’re a drama teacher looking for a fun exercise or a theatre enthusiast seeking a unique game for your next gathering, “Character Swap” is sure to spark creativity and laughter.

Objective:

The goal of “Character Swap” is to have participants step into the shoes of different characters, exploring various emotions and mannerisms while maintaining a coherent scene.

Instructions:

1. Gather a group of participants, preferably at least four or more to make the game more interesting.

2. Prepare slips of paper with the names of different characters, professions, or famous personalities written on them. Ensure there is one slip for each participant.

3. Fold the slips and place them in a hat or container.

4. Have each participant draw a slip and keep it secret from the others.

5. In a circle, designate one participant to start the scene as a character of their choice, inspired by the slip they drew.

6. The participants will then engage in a scene, with each person embodying the character on their slip. For example, if someone drew “Shakespearean actor,” they might speak in iambic pentameter and use old English expressions.

7. At any point during the scene, another participant can shout “Character Swap!” This prompts everyone to switch characters immediately, taking on the persona of the character on their new slip.

8. Continue the scene, allowing “Character Swap” moments to occur spontaneously. Encourage participants to adapt quickly and maintain the scene’s coherence.

9. The game ends when you feel the participants have had sufficient practice or when the scene naturally reaches a conclusion.

Tips:

– Encourage participants to fully embrace their assigned characters, including voice, posture, and mannerisms.

– Remind everyone that the objective is to keep the scene engaging and entertaining while exploring different characters.

– Be open to creativity and unexpected twists that arise from character swaps. Laughters guaranteed!

Conclusion:

“Character Swap” is a dynamic and entertaining drama game that promotes quick thinking, character development, and improvisational skills. It’s a fantastic way to challenge your actors or have a great time with younger students while exploring the world of drama. Give it a try and watch your participants transform into various, fascinating characters, creating memorable and hilarious scenes along the way.

Enjoy the game, and stay tuned for more drama game inspiration. Have a fantastic week!

Aurora xx
