Drama game of the week: Emotional Relay

Aurora Theatre — 26 Oct 2023

Welcome back to our Drama Game of The Week section!

Engage Your Students with This Week’s Fun Activity-  Looking for a new game to play with your students?

Try this one out! It’s called the Emotional Scene Circle.

Actors form a circle and one person starts by performing a brief emotional scene. It can be an excerpt from a scene or monologue they know by heart, or they can improvise a character on the spot.

They then tag the next actor, who must continue the scene with a different emotion.

This game is a great way for actors to explore a range of emotions while having fun! And don’t forget to remind your students about vocal variations, choices and possibilities!

Have fun, and see you next week!
