The Acting Chronicles (Bonus content): AI for actors

Aurora Theatre — 12 Mar 2024

Hello everyone!

We promised a little bonus content, but we were busy creating our new face-to-face acting course. Apologies for keeping you waiting. Anyway, here we are 🙂

Whether we like it or not, AI has entered our reality and is revolutionizing how we operate daily, from writing content to streamlining casting processes. A lot of controversy has spiked up in recent months about AI being able to replace many jobs, acting included. Before we get too worried about it, let’s take a breath and step back.

First, there are multiple reasons why AI won’t be able to replace humans (read here why not); AI is not a tool that expresses empathy or creativity as humans do.  It’s a valid asset that can assist individuals in enhancing a certain set of skills further and faster.

Second, let’s think about the Photoshop example for a minute: do photographers and visual artists use it?

Absolutely.

Does it take away from their art?

Absolutely not.

Photoshop doesn’t replace the work of a photographer/artist. It elevates their creations to a higher level contributing to a more complete work of art. Why shouldn’t be doing the same with AI then?

Since it’s easy to see that AI is here to stay, we can all appreciate that implementing it in our daily lives to our full advantage might be better than going against it.

But before we do that, we need to understand what can be done with it.

This week, to help you understand how to implement AI in your acting life, we are guiding you through our extra content to master the perks of AI and help you shine brighter as an actor.

Character Analysis: AI can make actors stronger text detectives. AI-powered tools analyze scripts and provide actors with valuable insights into their characters’ motivations, emotions, and behaviors. How many times, despite analyzing our scene multiple times, have we missed something that was not obvious to us, but was to somebody else who watched us? Thanks to this deep understanding, actors can deliver more authentic and compelling performances. Tools like ScriptBook  and SoCreate (amazing tool for writers as well- both green and seasoned) analyze scripts to provide insights into characters, story arcs, and emotional beats, helping actors better understand their roles.

Performance Feedback: As actors, it is not always easy to get feedback (especially for those who want to enter drama school).  Lots of institutions require a fee for you to apply and, once they receive your tape, they will watch it without you receiving any comment. And it’s not just something that happens only at drama school: it happens with the majority of productions and casting directors out there. You will only hear from them if you can make it through the next phase.  AI algorithms solve the issue by offering real-time feedback on actors’ performances, pinpointing areas for improvement such as delivery, timing, and expression, allowing actors to continuously refine their craft and elevate their performances. Check out services like WeAudition (an awesome platform that connects you to a community, as well as acting coaches, casting directors, and industry experts.) and RehearsalPro (offers a huge help when learning lines- comes with one payment only of 15 GBP, but well worth it.) to understand what we are talking about 😉

Accent and Dialect Training: This is a great option for those actors who are on a budget. AI-based language learning platforms assist actors in mastering different accents and dialects through voice recognition technology and personalized coaching. Apps like The Accent Kit (not an AI tool per se but it does offer vocal recognition and feedback) and ElsaSpeak (super helpful for non- native speakers) can help actors to refine their pronunciation and linguistic skills with precision through personalized feedback.

Virtual Rehearsal Spaces: Virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) technologies create immersive rehearsal environments where actors can practice scenes and interactions. This innovative approach to rehearsals allows for greater flexibility and creativity in the acting process. Check out VirtualActing for an immersive virtual rehearsal experience.

Casting Optimization: AI algorithms analyze casting calls, audition tapes, and actors’ profiles to help casting directors identify the best fit for roles. This streamlines the casting process, saving time and resources while ensuring to select the right talent for each project. Check out Cast It Talent and Casting Frontier: through AI-powered technology, they analyze audition tapes and match actors with the most suitable roles.

Content Creation: This, in our humble opinion, is a huge support for those actors/writers out there who struggle to find the time to write and to get ideas organized. Don’t get us wrong: we are not saying that AI should be writing on your behalf, but it can be a great tool to organize and elevate your writing to the next level. Tools like Sudowrite, ShortlyAI

and Cinematica help users in different kinds of ways: they can generate ideas, develop plots further, and point out inconsistencies in scripts. We strongly believe these are great resources for keeping your writing work going and developing it to the fullest.

Facial Recognition and Emotion Analysis: Thanks to AI technology it is now possible to analyze facial expressions and emotions, helping actors elevate their performance.  Tools like Affectiva use a type of technology that analyses facial recognition elevating actors’ performances to a more authentic and deeper delivery.

Costume and Set Design: Actors with a knack for visual arts can find some solid help as AI algorithms can suggest designs that align with any project’s vision. Artificial Intelligence in Design (AID) is a great tool for generating ideas for set and costume designs, while RunwayML allows users with more technical knowledge to create and experiment with AI models for various creative applications.

Audience Engagement: Those actors looking at enhancing audience engagement and the overall theatrical experience have various options to choose from. Platforms like Hootsuite, Sprout Social and Brandwatch offer social media listening capabilities and allow actors to monitor conversations, trends, and audience sentiment related to their work. On the other hand, platforms like Twitch, YouTube Live and Instagram Live allow actors to connect with their audience in real-time through live streaming. Why not create communities and platforms by hosting Q&A sessions, behind-the-scene tours, or interactive performances? Your audience will know about your latest news  (it’s also a great way to sell tickets, too). For those who host workshops and webinars, platforms like Gather and Hopin allow creating and hosting online meet-and-greets, workshops, and even virtual performances, offering the opportunity for actors to engage with new and existing audiences in new and unique ways.

Career Development: Every actor out there can use some guidance on their career and direction; AI platforms can offer plenty of advice, like Backstage and Casting Networks. With AI technology though they can offer actors personalized career guidance, recommending auditions, workshops, and networking opportunities based on their skills and goals. These are great tools that empower actors to navigate the industry more effectively and achieve higher status in their careers.

Yes- you guessed it right: as AI continues to evolve, its impact on the acting industry will only grow stronger. Don’t run away from it but embrace it to your full advantage. You may or may not remember when the internet first approached our world, but there was a high degree of skepticism mixed with the excitement that this novelty brought. Fast forward to thirty years later, could you imagine your life without the internet?

We can’t. So there you go then.

AI is revolutionizing every aspect of the acting process and it is empowering actors to grow to their full creative potential like never before. Get ready for the big jump, step into it and up. Who’s ready for this?

Have a beautiful week!

Team Aurora
