Drama game of the week: Soundscapes

Aurora Theatre — 16 Nov 2023

And we are back! How’s your week going?

We are excited for the weekend and our drama classes ahead. Need a  game that stimulates creativity and improvisation, and emphasizes the value of active listening and teamwork? Stay on this page: we’ve got you covered. Through our drama game of the week your students will develop creativity, improvisation, and teamwork by creating a collaborative audio story.

We have played this activity many times in our classroom with both youngsters and adults, and we guarantee you that every time was an absolute blast.

Here’s what to do:

Gather participants into a circle. Each person will contribute to building a collective soundscape.

Set the Scene: decide on a theme or setting for the soundscape. It could be a jungle, a bustling city, a beach, etc.

Assign Roles: assign different sound elements to participants. For example, one person could be responsible for animal sounds, another for environmental sounds, and so on.

Improvise Sounds: without speaking, participants take turns contributing their assigned sounds to the group. Please encourage them to be imaginative and use their bodies or simple props to create the sounds.

Dynamic Changes: as the story progresses, introduce changes in the soundscape. Prompt participants to adjust their sounds to reflect a shift in the environment or storyline.

Conclude the Soundscape: after a set period, gradually wind down the soundscape to bring the story to a close. The facilitator can guide this process by signaling participants to reduce their sounds.

Reflection: discuss the experience as a group. How did participants contribute to the collaborative soundscape? What challenges and successes did they encounter?

Remember to encourage participants to listen actively and respond to the sounds around them, and emphasize the importance of building upon each other’s contributions to create a cohesive soundscape.

Feel free to share your thoughts on the game’s effectiveness and any memorable moments with us!
