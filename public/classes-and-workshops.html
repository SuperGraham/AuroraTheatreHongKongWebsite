Join Our Acting Classes in Hong Kong

Join actress, director, playwright and acting coach Nicole Garbellini for a series of practical, engaging acting classes designed to build confidence, creativity and authentic performance.

Fresh from developing and producing her original play Unspoken: Permission Not Granted in Riyadh, Nicole returns to Hong Kong with a renewed focus on the craft that sits at the heart of every great performance: storytelling.

Whether you're completely new to acting or looking to deepen your skills, these classes will help you:

  • ● Develop confidence on stage and in everyday life
  • ● Strengthen creativity and spontaneity through improvisation
  • ● Explore character and scene work through scripts
  • ● Improve listening, communication and collaboration
  • ● Connect with a supportive community of actors and storytellers

About Nicole

Founder & Artistic Director

Nicole Garbellini is the founder and Artistic Director of Aurora Theatre. She is an actress, director, playwright and acting coach who has worked internationally across theatre, film, education and creative development. Her work combines professional acting techniques with soft-skills training, helping students become more confident, creative and connected performers.

From The Archive

Community & School Workshops

In 2016 we presented the children's workshop and performance of Shake, Shake, Shakespeare! in collaboration with local publisher Paddifield, culminating in a performance at Hong Kong Book Fair.

In 2017, we collaborated with I AM for Love Pings, a spicy and sexy comedy written by Kate March and directed by Nicole Garbellini, presented in Singapore and Hong Kong.

We've helped local charity The Rock Foundation produce and present their talent show, showcasing the talent of its students and volunteers alongside local actors and performers.

Aurora Theatre creates theatre, develops confident communicators and delivers unforgettable experiences for schools, businesses and audiences.
