Private Coaching

One-on-One Acting Coaching

We're offering private online one-on-one acting coaching. Get in touch for timing and prices.

Book A Session

Whether you are a seasoned actor looking to polish up your craft or you've just embarked on your acting journey, coaching is crucial to give you the tools that you need. Acting is not easy — it doesn't come immediately naturally to everyone. One must be dedicated and committed to embark on the journey with an open heart, and a great coach too.

Self-taping is now a fact of auditioning life, and as you master the art of auditioning on video, you'll inevitably train for this during your private sessions.

How It Works

  1. 1. Book your session via our contact form.
  2. 2. You'll receive a confirmation email.
  3. 3. Reply with your acting résumé, headshots, and anything you'd like to focus on (we provide scripts).
  4. 4. We'll be in touch about timing and links — sessions run on Zoom, FaceTime or Skype.

Consider starting with one session and see if it works out for you.

About The Coach

A theatre actress, director and producer, Nicole Garbellini has been in the theatre world for over 20 years. She was recently seen in Mussolini's Mistresses, as well as Silent Sky, The Complete Works of William Shakespeare (Abridged) and Love Pings. Her directing credits include The 39 Steps, Inherit The Wind, The Crucible, Dial "M" For Murder and Love Pings. Originally from Italy, she has worked and performed as an artist in Italy, Australia, Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau and Singapore.

You want to be a better actor? Better Call Nicole!
