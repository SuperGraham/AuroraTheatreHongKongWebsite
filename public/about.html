About Us

A Creative Partner, Not Just A Theatre Company

Aurora Theatre was founded in Hong Kong in 2010 by Nicole Garbellini with a vision of using theatre to bring people together.

Over the years Aurora has expanded from producing stage performances into delivering educational programmes, immersive entertainment, corporate training and communication coaching.

Today Aurora collaborates with schools, businesses, hospitality venues and community organisations to create memorable experiences through storytelling.

The Aurora Way

Theatre Has Never Been The End Goal. It's The Tool.

At Aurora Theatre, we believe that every meaningful interaction begins with a story. Whether we're directing a play, coaching a nervous presenter, training teachers, or designing an immersive dinner experience, we aren't simply creating performances. We are creating connection.

We use the principles of theatre — storytelling, empathy, collaboration, creativity and communication — to help people engage with one another in meaningful ways. For some, that means standing on a stage for the very first time. For others, it means leading a board meeting with confidence, inspiring a classroom of students, or creating an unforgettable evening for restaurant guests. The context changes, but the principles don't.

What We Believe

Stories connect people.

We believe stories help us understand ourselves and one another. They build empathy, inspire action and create lasting memories.

Learning should be experienced.

People remember what they do, not just what they hear. That's why every Aurora workshop is practical, engaging and interactive.

Confidence grows through practice.

Whether on stage, in a classroom or in a boardroom, confidence isn't something people are born with. It's something they build through experience.

Creativity belongs everywhere.

Drama isn't only for actors. Creative thinking, adaptability and communication are essential skills in education, business and everyday life.

Every production we stage, every workshop we deliver and every event we create begins with the same question: how can we create an experience that people will remember?

Our Mission

Aurora Theatre aims to attract different people, both familiar and unfamiliar with the local English theatre scene, and introduce them to the various shows presented by us — creating a robust, valid, creative and professional art scene in Hong Kong. We welcome people interested in learning drama in Hong Kong from every background, ethnicity and dialect. As we usually say, the more the variety, the better!

Our Charity Involvement

Aurora Theatre has used its shows for fundraising to support the victims of the Japanese and Italian earthquakes (2011, 2012 and 2016), as well as Cambodian children in need through Angel For Orphans. We continually look for fundraising associations to support.
