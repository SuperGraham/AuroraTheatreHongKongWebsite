About Us

The Best Place to Watch Acting in Hong Kong

Aurora Theatre Hong Kong is an independent theatre company that aims to promote English-language shows among the local Hong Kong art scene. Founded by theatre performer and producer Nicole Garbellini in 2010, Aurora Theatre Hong Kong has been very active since then and has brought international and original material to the stage. The theatre is the best place to showcase acting in Hong Kong.

Watch the Aurora Theatre story: Inherit the Wind and more →
Our Mission

Aurora Theatre aims to attract different people, both familiar and unfamiliar with the local English theatre scene, and introduce them to the various shows presented by us — creating a robust, valid, creative and professional art scene in Hong Kong. We aim to give opportunities to those individuals who are passionate about theatre, helping them stimulate and nurture ideas as far as performing and directing are concerned. Aurora Theatre welcomes people interested in learning drama in Hong Kong from every background, ethnicity and dialect. As we usually say, the more the variety, the better!

Our Charity Involvement

Aurora Theatre has used its shows for fundraising to support the victims of the Japanese and Italian earthquakes (2011, 2012 and 2016), as well as Cambodian children in need through Angel For Orphans. Through our generous supporters, we've been able to raise a significant amount of funds helping the needy throughout Asia, and we continually look for fundraising associations to support.
