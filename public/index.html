About Aurora

Independent. International. Hong Kong.

Aurora Theatre Hong Kong is an independent theatre company that aims to promote English-language shows among the local Hong Kong art scene. Founded by theatre performer and producer Nicole Garbellini in 2010, we've been very active since then, bringing international and original material to the stage.
