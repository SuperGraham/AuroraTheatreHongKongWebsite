Past Productions

Since 2010, Aurora Theatre has brought international and original material to stages across Hong Kong.

Dial "M" For Murder

Tony Wendice has married his wife, Margot, for her money and now plans to murder her for the same reason. He blackmails a scoundrel into strangling her for a fee, arranging a brilliant alibi for himself — but the murderer ends up murdered, and the victim survives.

Glengarry Glen Ross

From lies and flattery to bribes, threats and intimidation, as well as burglary — property agents engage in unethical and illegal acts, trying to sell undesirable real estate to unwitting prospective buyers.

Coffee and Cigarettes

Ever put the world to rights over coffee and cigarettes? In this evening of short plays, our characters do just that as they explore many themes — sometimes hilarious, sometimes intense.

Talking With

Idiosyncratic characters amuse, move and frighten, always speaking from the depths of their souls — a baton twirler, a fundamentalist snake handler, an ex-rodeo rider and an actress willing to go to any length to get a job.

Champagne and Cigarettes

A collection of ten-minute plays with an edge, united by two decadent pleasures. A night of comedy, laughs and tears, written by local and international playwrights.

The Last Days of Judas Iscariot

Set in a time-bending, darkly comic world between heaven and hell, this play re-examines the plight and fate of the New Testament's most infamous and unexplained sinner. Winner: Best Play, Best Director and Best Supporting Actor at the HKELD Awards, 2013.

Duetto — The Maid Made Mistress and Johnny XI

Pergolesi's La Serva Padrona and Puccini's Gianni Schicchi translated into comedies for the stage and re-set in Hong Kong — two classic tales, as funny and relevant as ever. No knowledge of opera required to enjoy the stories they're based on.

Bittergirl

Bittergirl charts the break-ups of three women — one single and dating, one co-habiting, one married with a child. This trio of Women Scorned experience heartbreak, hilarity, fairy-tales gone wrong and meetings of Breakups Anonymous.

Den of Thieves

The story of a shoplifter named Maggie looking to change her life. She and her sponsor Paul Handleman end up at the mercy of Louie "Little Tuna" — tied to chairs, they must fight for their lives by out-arguing each other as to who deserves to live.

Don Yuan

Donald Yuan has led a life of elite universities, fast cars and women. But this life has caught up with him, and Don Yuan is in court — his past comes back to haunt him, in more ways than one.

Romance

Pulitzer Prize-winning playwright David Mamet's Romance is an uproarious, take-no-prisoners courtroom comedy that gleefully lampoons everyone from lawyers and judges to Arabs, Jews, gays and chiropractors.

View Photo Gallery →

Thinner Than Water

Martin is deep in debt, addicted to gambling and dying. His three estranged children couldn't care less. As the world around them crumbles, they argue with each other in a desperate struggle to mend their rapidly deteriorating lives. Winner: Best Actress, HKELD Awards 2015.

Figaro

With humour and satirical commentary, de Beaumarchais's "The Barber of Seville" was an immediate sensation across Europe in the late 1700s. Figaro resets the play in modern Hong Kong, where classic French farce meets Asia's world city.

Inherit the Wind

Set during the infamous Scopes trial, this thought-provoking play pits the fundamentalist viewpoint against the advocate for science and freedom of thought. Winner: Best Play, Best Actor and Best Supporting Actor, HKELD Awards 2016.

View Photo Gallery →

The Last 5 Years

Jason Robert Brown's musical explores the five-year relationship between Jamie Wellerstein, a rising novelist, and Cathy Hiatt, a struggling actress — Cathy's story told in reverse, Jamie's in order. Winner: Best Actress, HKELD Awards 2016.

View Photo Gallery →

The Crucible

Arthur Miller's edgy masterpiece explores the threshold between individual guilt and mass hysteria in 1692 Salem, where a vengeful teenager's accusations of witchcraft multiply to consume an entire village. Winner: Best Supporting Actress and Best Costume Design, HKELD Awards 2017.

View Photo Gallery →

The Complete Works of William Shakespeare (Abridged)

37 Shakespearean plays in 97 minutes! A hilarious, fast-paced romp through the Bard's work that promises waves of laughter from beginning to end. Winner: Best Actor, HKELD Awards 2017.
